1Introduction

Welcome to KampusWave.

KampusWave is a developing campus connectivity initiative focused on understanding students' internet needs and exploring ways to make affordable Wi-Fi more accessible around the Lagos State University of Education (LASUED).

Your privacy matters to us. This Privacy Policy explains how KampusWave collects, uses, stores, shares, and protects your personal information when you visit our website, join our waitlist, or contact us.

KampusWave is currently in its planning and validation stage. Joining our waitlist does not mean that Wi-Fi service is already available or that a subscription has been purchased.

2Information We Collect

Depending on how you interact with our website, we may collect the following information:

Information you provide

  • Full name.
  • WhatsApp or phone number.
  • Email address, if provided.
  • Preferred campus location, such as student hostels, lecture areas, libraries, or student centres.
  • Information about your internet needs, estimated monthly data spending, and interest in a proposed subscription.
  • Any additional information you voluntarily send us through our contact channels.

Technical information

Our website hosting provider and essential website services may process limited technical information, such as IP address, browser type, device information, and server logs, where necessary for security, troubleshooting, and website operation.

We will only collect information that is reasonably necessary for the stated purposes.

3How We Use Your Information

We may use your information to:

  • Register and manage your waitlist submission.
  • Understand where students need internet access most.
  • Estimate student demand and evaluate the feasibility of a proposed campus Wi-Fi service.
  • Contact you about KampusWave's development, availability, or relevant updates where permitted.
  • Respond to questions and provide customer support.
  • Improve our website and the proposed service.
  • Protect our website against spam, misuse, and security threats.
  • Meet applicable legal and regulatory obligations.

We will not use your information for unrelated purposes without an appropriate lawful basis.

4Our Legal Basis for Processing

We will process personal information only where an appropriate lawful basis applies under relevant data protection law.

Depending on the circumstances, this may include your consent, steps you request us to take, legitimate interests where legally permitted, or compliance with a legal obligation.

Where we rely on consent, you may withdraw it. Withdrawal does not affect processing that was lawful before you withdrew consent.

5How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information.

We may share limited information with trusted service providers who help us operate the website, store waitlist submissions, communicate with users, or maintain website security. These providers should only process information as necessary for their authorised services and subject to appropriate safeguards.

We may also disclose information where required by law or where necessary to protect our rights, users, or the security of the platform.

If KampusWave later works with an internet service provider or technology partner, we will not assume that your waitlist details can automatically be shared with them. Any sharing must have an appropriate purpose, lawful basis, and applicable notice or consent where required.

6Data Storage and Retention

We will retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfil the purposes described in this Policy, comply with applicable legal obligations, resolve disputes, and maintain appropriate records.

Waitlist information may be deleted or anonymised when it is no longer needed for demand validation, service planning, or relevant communications.

The actual retention period will depend on the type of information, the reason it was collected, and any applicable legal requirements.

7How We Protect Your Information

We intend to use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards to protect personal information against unauthorised access, loss, alteration, disclosure, or misuse.

These measures may include restricted access, secure account management, appropriate hosting security, and secure transmission of information where supported.

No website or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. We will take appropriate steps to address security incidents in accordance with applicable law.

8Cookies and Similar Technologies

Our website may use essential browser storage or cookies to remember preferences, such as your light or dark theme, and to support basic website functionality.

Our hosting provider or other integrated services may also use technical mechanisms necessary to deliver and secure the website.

If we introduce analytics, advertising, or other non-essential tracking technologies, we will update this Policy and provide any choices or consent mechanisms required by applicable law.

You can manage cookies through your browser settings, although disabling certain technologies may affect some website features.

9Your Privacy Rights

Subject to applicable law and any relevant limitations, you may have the right to:

  • Request information about how we use your personal data.
  • Ask for access to personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Request deletion of your personal information where applicable.
  • Object to or request restriction of certain processing.
  • Request a copy of eligible personal information in a portable format.
  • Withdraw consent where processing relies on consent.
  • Lodge a complaint with the relevant data protection authority.

To exercise a right, contact us using the details in the Contact Us section. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

10Children's Privacy

KampusWave is intended for university students and other individuals interested in campus connectivity. Our waitlist is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate authorisation or another lawful basis.

If you believe a child has provided personal information to us inappropriately, contact us so we can review the situation and take suitable action.

11Links to Other Websites

Our website may contain links to external websites or services. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of websites operated by third parties.

Please review the relevant privacy policy before providing personal information to an external service.

12Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as KampusWave develops, our data practices change, or applicable legal requirements evolve.

When we make changes, we will update the “Last updated” date on this page. Where required, we will provide additional notice or seek renewed consent.
